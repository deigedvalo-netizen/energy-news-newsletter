Refined Products

Diesel is the tightest market in today's coverage. UK diesel passed £2 a litre, Russia extended its diesel export ban, and the US has pressed Europe to release reserves while threatening a US export ban. Developments like these are usually read as signs that distillate supply is constrained and that policy headlines can move the market quickly. Whether the US follows through on an export ban is still open, as is how quickly European stocks are released.
