# ADR-013: Carbon Credits without a price source

- **Status:** SUPERSEDED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-18

## Context

The owner added carbon credits. Daily EUA (ICE Endex/EEX) and CCA prices are exchange-licensed with no free, redistributable series comparable to EIA spot data.

## Decision

Add CARBON_CREDITS to the taxonomy, registry, tabs and synopsis with benchmark null. FeedBuilder emits price_effect status NO_PRICE_SOURCE with an explanatory note; relevance uses |z| = 0; the tab and the analysis state that no price effect is shown. Adding a licensed series later only requires a PriceSource adapter and a benchmark entry in settings. SUPERSEDED on 2026-09-16 by ADR-016: all price data is deferred, so Carbon Credits is simply a news commodity like the others.

## Consequences

Positive: carbon news, trends and analysis ship now without inventing prices. Negative: carbon stories rank lower on relevance than priced commodities with large moves, and carbon analysis cannot reference market moves.
