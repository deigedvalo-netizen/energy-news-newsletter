# ADR-005: Daily event-window price effect

- **Status:** DEPRECATED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-8, NFR-9

## Context

Free intraday data is not reliably licensable, and many stories break on the same day, so attributing moves to a single story overstates causality.

## Decision

Use daily settlements: event day T from the per-benchmark settlement time and holiday calendar; report day_move, two_day_move and z against sigma20. Missing prices produce PRICE_MISSING with no interpolation; clusters sharing T and benchmark are marked CONFOUNDED. Every badge is labelled 'correlated, not causal'. DEPRECATED on 2026-09-16: price data deferred (ADR-016); reinstate when prices.enabled is turned on.

## Consequences

Positive: honest, reproducible numbers from free data. Negative: coarse resolution; lagged EIA series leave some effects PENDING for days.
