# ADR-008: Flag rather than guess

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-3, FR-4, FR-6, FR-15, FR-19

## Context

Undated articles, unmatched selectors, unverified companies and conflicting figures are routine.

## Decision

FlagRegistry and the validators record typed flags (UNDATED, SELECTOR_NO_MATCH, NOT_RELEVANT, CONFLICTING_FIGURES, UNVERIFIED_COMPANY, UNVERIFIED_NUMBER); affected items are excluded or rendered with explicit markers, never filled with defaults.

## Consequences

Positive: data-quality problems are visible to the owner. Negative: some days look sparse.
