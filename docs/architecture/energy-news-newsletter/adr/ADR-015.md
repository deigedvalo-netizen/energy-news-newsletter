# ADR-015: Trends are ideas multiple companies push, tracked by an append-only ledger with computed trajectory

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-7, FR-16, FR-19, NFR-5

## Context

The owner defined a trend as 'an idea multiple companies are pushing, essentially trajectory'. Keyword topics cannot name ideas, and an LLM judging trajectory each day would be inconsistent. Options: (a) deterministic topic x commodity grouping; (b) the author decides trends and trajectory daily in prose; (c) the author records evidence in a ledger and code computes status and trajectory.

## Decision

Adopt (c). The pipeline supplies candidate groups (clusters naming 2+ companies) and the current trend views. The task appends dated mentions (company, story_url, short action) to issues/trends.json. The validator enforces append-only mentions whose URLs exist in retained snapshots and verifies each company against the story's digest text or organization tags. TrendEngine computes CONFIRMED (2+ verified companies in 30 days) vs EMERGING, NEW/RISING/STEADY/FADING/DORMANT from current vs previous 7-day windows, and hotness; the newsletter's hottest topics must be CONFIRMED.

## Consequences

Positive: trends are evidence-backed, trajectories are consistent day to day, and history accumulates for growth. Negative: trend quality depends on the author naming ideas well; company verification misses companies mentioned only in article bodies (kept as UNVERIFIED_COMPANY).
