# ADR-006: Terms-of-use gate, excerpt-only persistence and reference-only sources

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-1, FR-3, FR-18, NFR-4

## Context

Many high-value outlets are paywalled or prohibit automated use. Some valuable specialist sites, notably CDR.fyi, forbid scraping and AI summarization of their data without a commercial license (Terms of Service section 5.3, checked 2026-09-16).

## Decision

SourceRegistry fetches only ALLOWED or HEADLINE_ONLY sources, default UNVERIFIED. REFERENCE_ONLY sources are rendered as attributed further-reading links and never requested by the pipeline or given to the author to summarize. Body text is processed in memory for ALLOWED sources only; persisted excerpts are at most 300 characters; every item links to the original.

## Consequences

Positive: compliant by construction and safe to grow. Negative: CDR.fyi's carbon removal purchase data is unavailable to the newsletter until a partner or commercial license is obtained.
