# ADR-004: Two-level model: clusters and topic x commodity trends

- **Status:** SUPERSEDED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-5, FR-7

## Context

'Most commonly reported trends' mixes single events and multi-day themes; counting raw articles rewards outlets that republish.

## Decision

Cluster articles per event and count distinct sources, weighted by tier and decayed by age; aggregate clusters into trends keyed by (topic, commodity). Priority HIGH requires the 80th percentile score and at least one tier-1 source. SUPERSEDED on 2026-09-16 by ADR-015 (a trend is an idea multiple companies push, tracked as a trajectory). Story clusters remain.

## Consequences

Positive: syndicated duplicates do not inflate trends; official data releases dominate priority. Negative: topic taxonomy granularity limits how specific a trend can be.
