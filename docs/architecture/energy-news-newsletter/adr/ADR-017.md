# ADR-017: Author may read linked articles; unverified numbers warn instead of reject

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Feature:** energy-news-newsletter
- **Requirements:** FR-14, FR-15, FR-17, NFR-3, NFR-5

## Context

To condense news well, the author needs more than headlines and short excerpts, so it opens some linked articles. Figures read there are not in the digest, so the earlier rule (reject any number absent from the snapshot) would block most good newsletters. The readership is small and the page is prefaced as unverified.

## Decision

The task may open at most 15 URLs that appear in the digest and lists them in frontmatter. The validator REJECTS unsourced URLs, missing disclaimer or unverified line, advisory or forecast language, hottest topics that are not CONFIRMED, missing sections and reading-budget overruns. It WARNS on numbers absent from the digest; such newsletters publish with a visible 'contains figures not machine-verified' note. Revisit this before opening the site to a wider audience.

## Consequences

Positive: richer newsletters that still cannot invent sources, trends or advice. Negative: a mis-read figure from an article can reach readers, flagged but not blocked.
