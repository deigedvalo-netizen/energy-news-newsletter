African LNG project development — NEW · TotalEnergies, ExxonMobil

TotalEnergies and partner Amni took a final investment decision on the Ima gas project offshore Nigeria, which will feed Nigeria LNG's Bonny Island export plant, according to LNG Prime. ExxonMobil is moving its long-delayed Rovuma LNG project in Mozambique forward too, inviting bids for land transportation services, per LNG Prime. Two majors committing fresh capital to African supply this week is enough for this newly confirmed trend.

LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — FADING · Venture Global, BP, Glencore, Equinor, Eni, Petronas

Venture Global expanded China Gas's offtake to 2.5 MMtpa across its Plaquemines and CP2 terminals under 20-year contracts starting in 2030 (Rigzone), while BP sold a further 5% of its Browse project to Osaka Gas, taking its East Asian buyer farm-down to 10% and its own stake to 34.33% (Rigzone). Only two companies signed this week versus five the week before, so the trend is cooling from its mid-September peak.

Corporate purchases of durable carbon removal — STEADY · Google, Salesforce, Japan Airlines

Google, as a member of the Symbiosis Coalition alongside Microsoft, Meta, McKinsey, Salesforce, REI and Bain, has helped structure more than 40 million tons of nature-based carbon removal offtakes using PPA-style, pay-as-you-go terms, per Trellis. Salesforce separately became a buyer from Brazil's Mombak reforestation fund. Big Tech's megaton-scale commitments have kept a steady pace over the past two weeks rather than accelerating or slowing.
