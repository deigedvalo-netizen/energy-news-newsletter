African LNG project development — NEW · TotalEnergies, ExxonMobil

TotalEnergies and partner Amni took final investment decision on the Ima gas project offshore Nigeria, which will feed Nigeria LNG's Bonny Island export plant (LNG Prime). ExxonMobil is advancing its own Rovuma LNG project in Mozambique, inviting bids for land transportation contractors (LNG Prime). Both moves show majors continuing to commit capital to African gas supply even as security and financing risks remain elsewhere on the continent.

LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — FADING · Venture Global, BP, Glencore, Equinor, Eni, Petronas

Activity has cooled from five companies active the prior week to two this week: Venture Global raised China Gas's offtake to 2.5 MMtpa under 20-year contracts running from 2030 (Rigzone), while BP sold a further 5% of its Browse project to Osaka Gas, taking its East Asian buyer farm-down to 10% (Rigzone). The pace of new Asia-facing supply signings appears to be easing after a burst of deals earlier in September.

Corporate purchases of durable carbon removal — STEADY · Google, Salesforce, Japan Airlines

Google, as a member of the Symbiosis Coalition alongside Microsoft, Meta, McKinsey and others, helped structure standardized offtake agreements now covering more than 40 million tons of nature-based carbon removal (Trellis). Salesforce separately became a buyer from Brazil's Mombak reforestation fund (Economic Times). Two companies were active this week, matching last week's pace — steady rather than accelerating corporate demand.

US LNG export capacity expansion — FADING · Woodside Energy, Sempra, Mubadala, Kimmeridge, Cheniere Energy

The trend's hotness score has fallen as the wave of expansion announcements gives way to quieter construction updates.
