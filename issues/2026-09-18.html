US LNG export capacity expansion — NEW · Mubadala, Kimmeridge, Cheniere Energy

Cheniere completed the seventh and final train of Corpus Christi Stage 3, making the Texas terminal the second-largest LNG export facility in the US. Kimmeridge's Caturus unit, backed by Mubadala, is separately pushing to nearly double Commonwealth LNG's planned capacity, and Sempra added a 20-year, ~0.8 mtpa supply deal with Petrobras for its Port Arthur Phase 2 project still under development.

LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — NEW · Eni, Petronas, Trafigura

Trading houses and producers keep locking in long-term Asian offtake: Trafigura signed an MOU with China's ENN, Eni inked a supply pact with India's BPCL, and Petronas agreed to Japan's first emergency government LNG deal. Today Equinor added a long-term SPA with Thailand's PTT and Glencore signed a long-term supply deal with China's Suntien Green Energy, suggesting Asian buyers are still adding to their contracted books even as the Hormuz disruption reshapes near-term flows.

Eastern Mediterranean gas development buildout — NEW · Eni, Halliburton

Eni is advancing its Cronos ultra-deepwater gas project off Cyprus, and Halliburton won a multi-year drilling, completions and automation contract tied to that development, pointing to continued oilfield-services commitment to the region.

Oil majors retreat from fuel retail forecourts — NEW · BP, Shell

BP and Shell continue to exit UK fuel retail after Essar's owner agreed to buy 118 UK petrol stations from the two majors, part of a broader pullback from forecourt ownership toward independents.
