### LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — NEW · Glencore, Equinor, Eni, Petronas, Trafigura, BP (trend: lng-asia-offtake)

Trading houses, majors and NOCs keep locking in Asian LNG offtake: Equinor signed a long-term supply deal with Thailand's PTT, Petronas is supplying Japan under its first emergency LNG procurement deal, and Trafigura inked a cooperation pact with China's ENN. BP added to the pattern today, selling a further 5% Browse LNG stake to Japan's [Osaka Gas](https://www.rigzone.com/news/bp_to_further_sell_down_browse_project_stake_to_osaka_gas-21-sep-2026-184659-article?rss=true), taking its East Asian buyer farm-down to 10% and unlocking new feed gas for the Karratha plant.

### US LNG export capacity expansion — NEW · Sempra, Mubadala, Kimmeridge, Cheniere Energy (trend: us-lng-export-expansion)

Cheniere completed the seventh and final train at [Corpus Christi Stage 3](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68144), making the Texas facility the second-largest LNG export terminal in the US, while Kimmeridge's Caturus unit unveiled a five-train expansion that would nearly double [Commonwealth LNG capacity to about 17.25 mtpa](https://lngprime.com/americas/kimmeridges-caturus-plans-to-almost-double-commonwealth-lng-capacity/196892). Sempra also locked in a 20-year supply deal with Petrobras from its [Port Arthur LNG project](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/petrobras-locks-in-20-year-us-supply-from-sempras-lng-project-in-lone-star-state), stacking both new capacity and long-term offtake commitments.

### Biochar carbon removal supply scale-up — NEW · Exomad Green, Carbonfuture, Biochar Industrial Group (trend: biochar-carbon-removal-scaleup)

Biochar producers keep expanding durable-removal supply: Exomad Green extended its partnership with Carbonfuture to more than 1.1 million tons of removal credits through 2035, and Biochar Industrial Group raised $1.5 million to scale biochar-as-a-service in Sub-Saharan Africa. No fresh company moves surfaced today, but the trend remains newly confirmed on last week's activity.

### Eastern Mediterranean gas development buildout — NEW · Eni, Halliburton (trend: eastern-med-gas-buildout)

Halliburton won a multi-year bundled contract for drilling, completions and automation on Eni's [Cronos ultra-deepwater gas field](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/halliburton-bags-enis-deepwater-bundle-as-eastern-mediterranean-gas-buildout-continues) off Cyprus, targeting first production in 2028 at a plateau of roughly 500 million cubic feet per day. TotalEnergies is set to market half the output, and gas will move via subsea pipeline to Egypt's Zohr and Damietta facilities for export to Europe — a sign the basin is drawing sustained oilfield-services investment beyond a single operator.

### Oil majors retreat from fuel retail forecourts — FADING · BP, Shell (trend: majors-retreat-fuel-retail)

No new BP or Shell forecourt divestments surfaced this week, and the trend's weekly company count fell to zero even as UK and US pump prices set records. The original move — BP's sale of 118 UK petrol stations to Essar — remains the only evidence so far.
