LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — NEW · Glencore, Equinor, Eni, Petronas, Trafigura

Five companies inked new LNG supply or cooperation pacts with Asian buyers this week. Glencore signed a long-term deal to supply a unit of China's Suntien Green Energy, and Equinor sealed a long-term sales and purchase agreement with a unit of Thailand's PTT. Eni added India's BPCL as a buyer, Petronas agreed to supply Japan's government under its first emergency LNG deal, and Trafigura signed an MOU with China's ENN — a cluster of deals as buyers scramble for supply amid the Hormuz disruption. Glencore · Equinor · Eni/BPCL · Petronas

US LNG export capacity expansion — NEW · Sempra, Mubadala, Kimmeridge, Cheniere Energy

Cheniere completed Corpus Christi Stage 3, making the Texas facility the second-largest LNG export terminal in the US. Sempra locked in a 20-year supply deal with Petrobras from its Port Arthur LNG Phase 2 project — roughly 0.8 million tonnes a year, per Sempra's own figures — while Kimmeridge's Caturus unit and partner Mubadala unveiled plans to nearly double Commonwealth LNG's capacity to about 17.25 mtpa. Cheniere/Corpus Christi · Sempra/Petrobras · Kimmeridge/Mubadala

Eastern Mediterranean gas development buildout — NEW · Eni, Halliburton

Halliburton won a multi-year drilling, completions and automation services contract on Eni's Cronos ultra-deepwater gas field off Cyprus, a sign that Eastern Mediterranean gas investment is continuing even as Middle East tensions raise the regional risk profile. Halliburton/Eni

Oil majors retreat from fuel retail forecourts — NEW · BP, Shell

BP and Shell continued pulling back from UK forecourts as Essar, owner of the Stanlow refinery, agreed to buy 118 petrol stations from the pair.
