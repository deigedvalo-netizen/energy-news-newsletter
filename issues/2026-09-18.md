## Hottest topics

### US LNG export capacity expansion — NEW · Mubadala, Kimmeridge, Cheniere Energy (trend: us-lng-export-expansion)

Cheniere completed the seventh and final train of Corpus Christi Stage 3, making the Texas terminal the [second-largest LNG export facility in the US](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68144). Kimmeridge's Caturus unit, backed by Mubadala, is separately pushing to [nearly double Commonwealth LNG's planned capacity](https://lngprime.com/americas/kimmeridges-caturus-plans-to-almost-double-commonwealth-lng-capacity/196892), and Sempra added a 20-year, ~0.8 mtpa supply deal with [Petrobras for its Port Arthur Phase 2 project](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/petrobras-locks-in-20-year-us-supply-from-sempras-lng-project-in-lone-star-state) still under development.

### LNG cooperation deals with Asian buyers — NEW · Eni, Petronas, Trafigura (trend: lng-asia-offtake)

Trading houses and producers keep locking in long-term Asian offtake: Trafigura signed an [MOU with China's ENN](https://lngprime.com/asia/chinas-enn-inks-lng-cooperation-pact-with-trafigura/196943), Eni [inked a supply pact with India's BPCL](https://lngprime.com/asia/indias-bpcl-inks-lng-pact-with-eni/196976), and Petronas agreed to [Japan's first emergency government LNG deal](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/japan-gas-association-welcomes-governments-first-emergency-lng-deal-petronas-6389061). Today Equinor added a [long-term SPA with Thailand's PTT](https://lngprime.com/contracts-and-tenders/equinor-seals-long-term-lng-spa-with-thailands-ptt/197098) and Glencore signed a [long-term supply deal with China's Suntien Green Energy](https://lngprime.com/asia/glencore-pens-chinese-lng-supply-deal/197109), suggesting Asian buyers are still adding to their contracted books even as the Hormuz disruption reshapes near-term flows.

### Eastern Mediterranean gas development buildout — NEW · Eni, Halliburton (trend: eastern-med-gas-buildout)

Eni is advancing its Cronos ultra-deepwater gas project off Cyprus, and [Halliburton won a multi-year drilling, completions and automation contract](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/halliburton-bags-enis-deepwater-bundle-as-eastern-mediterranean-gas-buildout-continues) tied to that development, pointing to continued oilfield-services commitment to the region.

### Oil majors retreat from fuel retail forecourts — NEW · BP, Shell (trend: majors-retreat-fuel-retail)

BP and Shell continue to exit UK fuel retail after Essar's owner agreed to [buy 118 UK petrol stations](https://www.ft.com/content/130f45b6-ee45-4fa0-83eb-5ea8f9bc4680?syn-25a6b1a6=1) from the two majors, part of a broader pullback from forecourt ownership toward independents.
